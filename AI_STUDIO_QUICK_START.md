# 🚀 AI Studio - Quick Start Guide

## Getting Started in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: Run the Application

```bash
cd client
npm run dev
```

The app will open at `http://localhost:5173`

### Step 2: Login or Sign Up

You need to be authenticated to access AI Studio.

### Step 3: Navigate to AI Studio

Click the **🤖 AI Studio** button in the navigation bar (it has a special yellow border highlight).

### Step 4: Explore the Features!

You'll see a beautiful sidebar with 6 AI-powered features. Click any feature to try it out.

---

## 🎯 Feature-by-Feature Quick Guide

### 1. AI Mirror Mode (⏱️ 30 seconds)

**What to do:**
1. Click "AI Mirror Mode" in the sidebar
2. Click "Start Mirror" button
3. Allow camera permissions when prompted
4. Click different season buttons (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter)
5. Watch your webcam feed transform with beautiful color filters!

**Best for:** Seeing how seasonal colors look on you in real-time

---

### 2. Virtual Closet (⏱️ 2 minutes)

**What to do:**
1. Click "Virtual Closet" in the sidebar
2. Click "Upload Clothes" button
3. Select 2-3 photos of your clothing items
4. Wait for AI to analyze the colors
5. See AI-suggested pairings below each item!

**Best for:** Organizing your wardrobe and getting outfit ideas

**Tip:** Use clear photos with good lighting for best results

---

### 3. AI Style DNA (⏱️ Instant)

**What to do:**
1. Click "AI Style DNA" in the sidebar
2. First time? It will show "No Style DNA Yet"
3. Use other features (Mirror, Closet, Outfit Generator)
4. Come back to see your style profile build automatically!

**Best for:** Understanding your unique style personality

**Note:** The more you use AI Studio, the more accurate your Style DNA becomes

---

### 4. AI Outfit Generator (⏱️ 1 minute)

**What to do:**
1. Click "AI Outfit Generator" in the sidebar
2. Select a season (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter)
3. Choose a mood (Casual, Formal, Artistic, Energetic)
4. Click "Generate Outfits"
5. Browse 3 unique outfit combinations with fashion sketches!

**Best for:** Getting inspired with new outfit ideas

**Tip:** Try different season + mood combinations for variety

---

### 5. AR Palette Glow (⏱️ 1 minute)

**What to do:**
1. Click "AR Palette Glow" in the sidebar
2. Click "Start AR" button
3. Allow camera permissions
4. Select different seasonal glows
5. Experience beautiful AR effects around you!

**Best for:** Mobile users wanting AR color experiences

**Tip:** Works best on mobile phones in good lighting

---

### 6. Palette Memory Wall (⏱️ 2 minutes)

**What to do:**
1. Click "Palette Memory Wall" in the sidebar
2. Click "Create Palette" button
3. Name your palette (e.g., "My Wedding Look")
4. Select a base season
5. Click "Create"
6. Your palette appears on the wall!
7. Click "Share" to copy a shareable link
8. Click the heart to mark as favorite

**Best for:** Saving and sharing your favorite color combinations

## 🎨 Pro Tips

### Get the Best Results

1. **Camera Features (Mirror & AR):**
   - Use in well-lit rooms
   - Face the camera directly
   - Try different backgrounds
   - Chrome/Edge browsers work best

2. **Virtual Closet:**
   - Upload clear, well-lit photos
   - One item per photo works best
   - Solid backgrounds preferred
   - Photos should focus on the clothing item

3. **AI Outfit Generator:**
   - Try all season + mood combinations
   - Each combination creates different styles
   - Use "Save" to keep your favorites

4. **Style DNA:**
   - The more features you use, the better it learns
   - Check back after using other features
   - Your percentages update automatically

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Camera Not Working?

**Problem:** "Unable to access camera" error

**Solutions:**
1. Check browser permissions (Click lock icon in URL bar)
2. Ensure you're using HTTPS or localhost
3. Try Chrome or Edge browsers
4. Close other apps using the camera
5. Refresh the page and try again

### Features Not Saving?

**Problem:** Data disappears after refresh

**Solutions:**
1. Ensure you're not in Incognito/Private mode
2. Check if localStorage is enabled
3. Clear browser cache and try again

### Slow Performance?

**Problem:** Camera features lag or freeze

**Solutions:**
1. Close other browser tabs
2. Ensure good internet connection
3. Use a device with better GPU
4. Lower camera quality by moving further from camera
